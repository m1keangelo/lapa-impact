# Photo Credits

All photographs in this directory are REAL imagery from the M7.4 earthquake of
10 August 2026 (epicenter near San José del Palmar, Chocó, Colombia) and its
relief response. Sources: World Central Kitchen (CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia
Commons) and the U.S. Geological Survey (public domain).

Images were resized to a maximum width/height of 1920 px; no other alteration
except where a source file was itself a published crop (noted below).
